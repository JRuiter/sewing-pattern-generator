## Fabric needed

- **Main fabric:** about 1.05 m at 150 cm width (single layer, incl. 10 % for shrinkage; folded would need 1.25 m)
- **Rib knit:** about 0.15 m at 70 cm width (single layer, incl. 10 % for shrinkage)
- Design: option hem_band = False (instead of True): needed for the shape.
- Design: option kangaroo = False (instead of True): needed for the shape.
- Creative design 'Starfish': the shape deviates from the base block on purpose; see 'Design' in the validation report.
- Shaped hem: finish it with a narrow hem (overlock or zigzag the edge, turn 1 cm, topstitch) or a facing; clip into every inner point.

Cutting layout: see `cutting_layout.png` (grainline arrow parallel to the selvage, "flipped" = place the piece face down, "×2" = cut through both layers of the folded fabric, which gives a mirrored pair, "(unfolded)" = cut-on-fold piece shown opened out).

**Cut on fold** (Front, Back): the paper piece is only one half. Its fold edge (dash-dot line, bracket arrows "PLACE ON FOLD") is the centre of the piece: it gets no seam allowance and is never cut. Where the cutting layout shows a piece "(unfolded)" on a single layer, fold the fabric along the grain just wide enough for the piece and place the fold edge on that fold, or trace the paper piece, flip it over along the fold edge and trace the second half.

## Sewing instructions

The printed instructions (`sewing_instructions.pdf`) show every step as a picture of your pieces: parts list, tools, then one action per step.

Seam allowance is included in the pattern (see table). Use a jersey/stretch needle for jersey and sweat.

1. **Pre-wash the fabric**. Wash and dry it the way you will wash the garment later, so it shrinks now, not after sewing.
2. **Cut out all pieces**. Follow the cutting layout; grainline arrows parallel to the selvage. Rib knit: the ribs run along the grainline, so every band stretches along its length (across the fabric). Single layer: cut the second piece of a pair with the paper piece turned over (printed side down).
3. **Snip the notches**. Cut 3–4 mm into the seam allowance at every notch. Tip: Never deeper than half the seam allowance.
4. **Set up the machine**. Sewing machine: jersey/stretch needle 75–80, polyester thread. Sew a test seam on two layers of a scrap. See the basics page at the front of the booklet.
5. **Sew the shoulder seams**. Lay front and back right sides together, shoulder edges level, and pin. Sew with 10 mm seam allowance from the neckline to the armhole. Press the allowances towards the back. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Do this twice. Knit stretches at the shoulder: sew a strip of clear elastic or twill tape into the seam.
   ![Step 5](steps/step_05.png)
6. **Sew the hood centre seam**. Lay the two hood pieces right sides together, curved back edges level, and pin from the neck edge upwards. Sew with 10 mm seam allowance. Press the allowances to one side. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Topstitch 6 mm beside the seam through the allowances: the seam stays flat.
   ![Step 6](steps/step_06.png)
7. **Hem the hood front edge**. Fold the front edge 2.5 cm to the wrong side along the fold line and press. Topstitch 2 cm from the fold, close to the raw edge; leave the ends open for a drawstring. (sewing machine: twin needle 4 mm, stitch 3 mm) Alternative: Lined hood (option hood_lining): no hem, the lining finishes the front edge and the hood is warmer.
   ![Step 7](steps/step_07.png)
8. **Sew the hood on**. Body opened out flat, right side up. Lay the hood right side down onto the neckline, neck edges level. Hood centre seam on centre back, hood notches on the shoulder seams; front edges meet at centre front (overlap 1 cm). Sew all round with 10 mm seam allowance. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch)
   ![Step 8](steps/step_08.png)
9. **Finish the neck seam**. Finish both allowances together. Press them down towards the body. (sewing machine: wide zigzag 4 × 1.5 mm over the edge) Alternative: Neatest finish: cover the allowance with a strip of jersey or cotton tape from shoulder to shoulder over the back neck and stitch it down on both edges.
   ![Step 9](steps/step_09.png)
10. **Set in the sleeves**. Open the body out flat, right side up. Lay the sleeve right side down on the armhole: top of the cap on the shoulder seam, single notch on the front, double notch on the back. Pin from the middle to both ends, easing in the extra length of the cap. Sew with 10 mm seam allowance, sleeve underneath; press the allowances towards the sleeve. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Do this twice. Tip: Sleeve on the bottom: the feed dogs ease the cap in without pleats.
   ![Step 10](steps/step_10.png)
11. **Sew side and sleeve seams**. Fold the garment right sides together at the shoulders, the sleeves folded in half lengthwise. Pin side and sleeve seam so that the armhole seams meet exactly at the underarm. Sew in one go with 10 mm seam allowance, from the hem over the underarm to the sleeve hem. Press the allowances towards the back. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Do this twice. Alternative: Cuffs first: sew the cuffs on while the sleeve is still flat, then sew side, sleeve and cuff seam in one go. Easier on small sizes.
   ![Step 11](steps/step_11.png)
12. **Close the cuffs into rings**. Fold each cuff crosswise right sides together, short edges level. Sew with 10 mm seam allowance; finger-press the allowances open. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Do this twice.
   ![Step 12](steps/step_12.png)
13. **Fold the cuffs in half**. Fold the ring lengthwise wrong sides together, raw edges level. The seam allowance is now inside; the right side shows all round. Do this twice.
   ![Step 13](steps/step_13.png)
14. **Sew the cuffs on**. Turn the sleeve right side out. Slide the cuff over the sleeve end, raw edges level, cuff seam on the sleeve seam; pin there and at the opposite folds of cuff and sleeve. Sew with the cuff on top, stretching the cuff (not the sleeve) to the length of the sleeve between two pins. (an overlocker or a narrow zigzag/stretch stitch) Do this twice. One hand in front of the presser foot, one behind it: both stretch evenly.
   ![Step 14](steps/step_14.png)
15. **Sew the hem**. Fold the hem 10 mm to the wrong side and press. Sew it down 6 mm from the fold, from the right side. (a twin needle/coverstitch) Tip: Shaped hem: clip into every inner point up to 2 mm before the fold and press each point separately.
   ![Step 15](steps/step_15.png)
16. **Final pressing**. Press all seams with steam; do not press down hard on sweat.
